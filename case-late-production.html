The challenge

The other side produced 40,000 documents with the hearing three weeks out. Four lawyers, no time for a linear review and no budget for a review team.

The approach

The team loaded the production into Cicero and worked from the matter model. Every proposition was pulled out and attached to the issues in dispute and the people who made it, with contradictions flagged.

What they found

Eighteen documented reasons their client should not give evidence, each traced to its source. That changed the strategy for the hearing.

The outcome

A multimillion dollar difference in the result, found by four people in three weeks in a production designed to bury them.
