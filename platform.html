The evolution of fact finding

Each generation of review technology narrowed what a human had to read. None of them built the case.

Under the hood

Every fact, filed where it belongs.

A witness statement isn't one thing. It's dozens of claims about who did what, and when. Cicero pulls out each one, works out which event it describes and which issue it goes to, and checks it against everything else the record says about that event.

StepLayerWhat Cicero does
01IssuesTurns the allegations and denials into a list of what is actually in dispute, and who is involved.
02PropositionsBreaks each document into its claims: who said what, when, where and to whom.
03EventsGroups claims about the same event, wherever they came from. When two accounts disagree, you see both.
04Work productDrafts the chronology, the issues and evidence matrices and the cast list, with a source on every line.

Chronology

Every date in dispute, and who disputes it.

Cicero groups every proposition into events and puts them in date order. Where accounts of an event differ, both sit on the entry with their sources. Export it as the schedule for the hearing.

01One entry per eventEverything the record says about an event sits on one entry, whichever documents it came from.
02Conflicts shownWhere two accounts differ, both appear, with the paragraph each one comes from.
03Cut by issueFilter to the events that matter for one issue, one person or one month.
04Any languageEntries draw on documents in every language in the record, each tied to its original.

The Agent

Ask the hard questions. Check every answer.

Built for evidence, not legal research. The Agent uses the matter model to decide which documents to read, so it works across the whole record rather than whatever a search happened to return.

01Plans firstHard questions get a plan you can edit, and an estimate of how long the answer will take.
02Shows its workingEvery citation can be checked, and it lists what it read but didn't cite.
03Takes outside materialAdd an authority or another document and test it against the evidence.
04Hands you a draftAsk for a Word document and you get one.
